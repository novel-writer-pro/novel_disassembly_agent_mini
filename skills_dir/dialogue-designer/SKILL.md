# Dialogue Designer

Use this skill after a draft exists and before final revision closure.

## Inputs
- Draft text
- Chapter goal
- Relationship / character constraint hints

## Outputs
- Dialogue issues
- Speaker differentiation hints
- Information-efficiency notes
- Recommended dialogue repair actions

## Rules
- Diagnose dialogue quality, do not rewrite the whole chapter.
- Focus on clarity, character voice separation, and conflict-carrying lines.
- Prefer concrete repair guidance over literary generalities.
