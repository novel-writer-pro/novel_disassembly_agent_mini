# Reader Sim Review

Use this skill after a draft exists and before final acceptance.

## Inputs
- Draft text
- Chapter goal
- Constraint pack

## Outputs
- Reader profile
- Engagement score
- Reader concerns
- Recommended reader-facing repairs

## Rules
- Simulate a reader, not an editor.
- Prefer “where will a reader get bored/confused/lose trust” over technical critique.
- Keep outputs structured and concise.
