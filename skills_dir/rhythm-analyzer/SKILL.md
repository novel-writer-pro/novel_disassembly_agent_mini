# Rhythm Analyzer

Use this skill after a draft or scene plan exists and before final gate review.

## Inputs
- Draft text
- Scene plan / scene beats
- Chapter goal

## Outputs
- Pace label
- Hook strength
- Tension curve
- Rhythm issues
- Recommended pacing repairs

## Rules
- Diagnose rhythm, do not rewrite prose directly.
- Prefer concrete scene-level guidance over abstract literary commentary.
- Flag weak hook / flat middle / abrupt escalation before stylistic complaints.
