# Research Pack

Use this skill before or during planning when a chapter needs richer setting or audience awareness.

## Inputs
- Chapter goal
- World / rule context
- Optional topic or era hints

## Outputs
- Setting notes
- Rule reminders
- Audience expectation notes
- Research caution points

## Rules
- Keep outputs compact and structured.
- Prefer actionable setting/audience guidance over essays.
- Avoid inventing unsupported external facts when no source is available; emit caution notes instead.
